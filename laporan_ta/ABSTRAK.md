### ABSTRAK (BAHASA INDONESIA)
Industri peternakan ayam broiler memiliki peranan krusial dalam pemenuhan protein hewani nasional, namun peternak rakyat sering kali mengalami kegagalan panen akibat keterlambatan mendeteksi penurunan kondisi lingkungan mikroklimat kandang. Fluktuasi temperatur dan kelembaban relatif (*Relative Humidity* / RH) yang tidak terkontrol berdampak langsung terhadap laju mortalitas dan pembengkakan rasio konversi pakan (*Feed Conversion Ratio* / FCR), yang bermuara pada rendahnya nilai Indeks Performa (IP). Evaluasi mikroklimat konvensional umumnya menggunakan ambang batas suhu statis tanpa mempertimbangkan umur ayam, padahal kebutuhan termal ayam bersifat dinamis seiring pertambahan biomassa tubuh dan kematangan termoregulasi. Penelitian ini bertujuan merancang dan mengimplementasikan Sistem Pendukung Keputusan (SPK) menggunakan *Fuzzy Inference System* (FIS) dengan variabel deviasi suhu adaptif umur ($\Delta T = T_{\text{aktual}} - T_{\text{target}}(Umur)$) berdasarkan standar pedoman strain komersial (Cobb 500 / Ross 308). Sistem dibangun menggunakan framework Flutter (Dart) yang berjalan 100% *offline* di perangkat Android untuk mendukung kondisi kandang tanpa sinyal internet. Penelitian ini membandingkan kinerja penalaran fuzzy metode **Mamdani (Centroid)** dan **Sugeno Orde-Nol (Weighted Average)** pada 50 skenario kasus uji representatif. Hasil pengujian menunjukkan bahwa metode Sugeno memiliki efisiensi komputasi yang jauh lebih tinggi dengan rata-rata waktu inferensi sebesar **0.0054 ms** (sekitar **92.5 kali lebih cepat** dibandingkan metode Mamdani sebesar **0.4959 ms**). Meskipun komputasinya jauh lebih ringan, keluaran skor Sugeno memiliki keselarasan sangat tinggi terhadap Mamdani dengan koefisien korelasi Pearson **$r = 0.9987$**, nilai *Mean Absolute Error* (MAE) sebesar **3.88 poin**, dan tingkat kesesuaian kategori klasifikasi mencapai **98.0%**. Aplikasi dilengkapi dengan fitur deteksi fase umur instan, panduan tindakan korektif berurutan prioritas (kipas blower, bukaan tirai, air minum), sistem peringatan dini (*early warning banner*) saat skor menurun tiga hari beruntun, serta grafik tren performa visual. Sistem ini diharapkan dapat menjadi sarana mitigasi dini yang aplikatif bagi peternak rakyat guna mempertahankan efisiensi produksi dan memaksimalkan Indeks Performa panen.

**Kata Kunci**: *Ayam Broiler, Fuzzy Inference System, Adaptif Umur, Indeks Performa, Mamdani, Sugeno, Flutter Offline.*

### ABSTRACT (ENGLISH)
*The broiler poultry industry plays a pivotal role in national animal protein supply; however, smallholder farmers frequently suffer financial losses due to delayed detection of microclimate deterioration. Uncontrolled fluctuations in ambient temperature and relative humidity directly increase mortality and inflate the feed conversion ratio (FCR), ultimately depressing the Performance Index (PI). Conventional microclimate assessments typically employ static temperature thresholds without adjusting for bird age, despite the dynamic nature of avian thermal requirements as body biomass increases and thermoregulatory capacity matures. This study designs and implements a Decision Support System (DSS) utilizing an age-adaptive Fuzzy Inference System (FIS) based on temperature deviation ($\Delta T = T_{\text{actual}} - T_{\text{target}}(Age)$) derived from commercial strain guidelines (Cobb 500 / Ross 308). The application is developed using the Flutter (Dart) framework operating 100% offline on Android devices to accommodate rural poultry farms lacking internet connectivity. A comparative benchmark between **Mamdani (Centroid)** and **Zero-Order Sugeno (Weighted Average)** fuzzy reasoning methods was conducted across 50 representative test scenarios. Empirical results demonstrate that the Sugeno method achieves superior computational efficiency with an average inference latency of **0.0054 ms** (approximately **92.5 times faster** than Mamdani at **0.4959 ms**). Despite its lower complexity, Sugeno outputs show near-perfect agreement with Mamdani, exhibiting a Pearson correlation coefficient of **$r = 0.9987$**, a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of **3.88 points**, and a **98.0%** category concordance rate. The mobile app integrates real-time age-phase detection, priority corrective action checklists (exhaust blowers, side curtains, drinking water), a three-day consecutive trend early warning banner, and visual score monitoring graphs. This system provides an accessible decision aid for smallholders to mitigate flock mortality and maximize harvesting performance.*

**Keywords**: *Broiler Poultry, Fuzzy Inference System, Age-Adaptive, Performance Index, Mamdani, Sugeno, Flutter Offline.*
